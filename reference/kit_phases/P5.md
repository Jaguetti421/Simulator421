## Exit evidence

- All STORY 01–12 scenes pass, including guest-disabled equivalence, interrupted persecution and exact Accord eligibility.
- Eight sourced themes and six encounters support voluntary diverse responses without LLM dependence.
- Guest state persists and records never contaminate Standard wins or competitor K/A totals.

The final P5 integration packet prepares templates/GATE_EVIDENCE.md with exact candidate hashes and actual reports. A failed criterion requires a repair packet followed by affected retests; a repair plan alone cannot pass. Use PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/HUMAN_REQUIRED honestly. Gate evidence is invalidated when later changes affect its behavior; A0 records the targeted revalidation before carrying a gate forward.
