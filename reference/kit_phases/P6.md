# P6 — Beta and release candidate

Exit gate: **GR** · 12 bounded packets · Not one chat session.

Close measured defects, prove recovery and performance, validate the first-run experience and deliver an offline Windows candidate.

## Entry and authorization

Requires accepted G4 and GS plus explicit P6 approval. Approval does not authorize public publishing.

A dependency must be ACCEPTED on the current integrated baseline before dispatch. One active packet per owner and at most four builders are the default. A0 may issue a disjoint wave only after contracts are integrated; A7 reviews returns and A0 verifies combined code. A7-authored tools are independently reviewed by A0. No automatic multi-agent spawning is required by this kit; Jani controls the chat windows.

## Exit evidence

- G4 and GS are accepted and all affected prior evidence is revalidated on the integrated candidate.
- All 51 scenes, release regression, fuzz/recovery/soak and full-population hardware checks pass.
- Eight-minute lesson, first-run flow and clean-PC offline Windows package work.
- Licenses/credits, source/executable manifests and known limitations are complete; public distribution still awaits its own instruction.

The final P6 integration packet prepares templates/GATE_EVIDENCE.md with exact candidate hashes and actual reports. A failed criterion requires a repair packet followed by affected retests; a repair plan alone cannot pass. Use PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/HUMAN_REQUIRED honestly. Gate evidence is invalidated when later changes affect its behavior; A0 records the targeted revalidation before carrying a gate forward.

## Stop and next step

Agents stop after their assigned packet, even when the next dependency is ready. A0 presents a reviewable phase candidate to Jani and asks explicit permission for the next phase/baseline. Resume of an incomplete authorized packet is allowed; beginning the next packet is a new dispatch. P6 approval never authorizes uploading or publishing the game. Optional PX may remain deferred without changing core scope.
