## Exit evidence

- All 100 identities and twelve action states meet day/night/dense-camp/finale readability at 100 and 150 percent text scale.
- All 300 core lines, action intents and supported sound/visual cues validate.
- Six factual threads, career dossiers, epithets, notes and safe offline Saga exports work.
- Human visual/readability review and content evidence are attached.

The final P4 integration packet prepares templates/GATE_EVIDENCE.md with exact candidate hashes and actual reports. A failed criterion requires a repair packet followed by affected retests; a repair plan alone cannot pass. Use PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/HUMAN_REQUIRED honestly. Gate evidence is invalidated when later changes affect its behavior; A0 records the targeted revalidation before carrying a gate forward.
