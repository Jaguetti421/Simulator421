# P2 — 24-person Trial

Exit gate: **G2** · 26 bounded packets · Not one chat session.

A full thirty-minute Trial adds clans, differentiated cooperation, all powers, durable records and truthful forecasts.

## Entry and authorization

Requires accepted G1 and explicit P2 approval. Real comprehension evidence is part of G2.

A dependency must be ACCEPTED on the current integrated baseline before dispatch. One active packet per owner and at most four builders are the default. A0 may issue a disjoint wave only after contracts are integrated; A7 reviews returns and A0 verifies combined code. A7-authored tools are independently reviewed by A0. No automatic multi-agent spawning is required by this kit; Jani controls the chat windows.

## Exit evidence

- Complete Trial runs with clans, powers, knowledge, differentiated rescue, careers and forecasts.
- Mapped GDD scenes, 100-seed Trial evidence and save/observer invariance pass.
- Ten real new viewers are tested; at least eight recall three contestants, explain two choices and understand the next law.
- Actual reference-hardware and operation-count evidence meets accepted Trial targets; missing evidence blocks closure.

The final P2 integration packet prepares templates/GATE_EVIDENCE.md with exact candidate hashes and actual reports. A failed criterion requires a repair packet followed by affected retests; a repair plan alone cannot pass. Use PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/HUMAN_REQUIRED honestly. Gate evidence is invalidated when later changes affect its behavior; A0 records the targeted revalidation before carrying a gate forward.

## Stop and next step

Agents stop after their assigned packet, even when the next dependency is ready. A0 presents a reviewable phase candidate to Jani and asks explicit permission for the next phase/baseline. Resume of an incomplete authorized packet is allowed; beginning the next packet is a new dispatch. P6 approval never authorizes uploading or publishing the game. Optional PX may remain deferred without changing core scope.
