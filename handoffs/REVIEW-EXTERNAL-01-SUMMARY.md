## What the plans got wrong, in light of the implementation

1. `AppearanceRecipe` cited as an existing contract (TP v2.0 §13) — it was a concept in v1.1 §13. Corrected wording: "new record, introduced in P1-01".
2. `LAW-NOTICE-REJECTION.json` asserted a `CommandRejected` **event**; rejections live on `CommandAck`. DEBT-01's ack-matching is the right resolution; the DSL should name the source (`AckCountGte`).
3. Cards W0-07/W0-10 and AGENTS.md said BLOCKED_RENDER in the sandbox; Playwright's Chromium installs and software WebGL renders. Replace with "record what the environment provides".
4. TP v2.0 §18 tick targets were pre-measurement guesses; the synthetic P95 is far under them but proves nothing about production load — keep the row marked "proposed" until P1 fixtures exist.
5. TP v1.1 §5 uses "region" and "portal" without defining them; the implementation chose tiles. The plan should say: regions are connected components of passable cells (size-capped); portals are passable boundary cells.
6. TP v2.0 §13 "one geometry hash for render and simulation" should have said "one *canonically serialized* hash" — the implementation's XOR combination satisfied the letter and not the intent.
7. Card W0-10's acceptance said "distinguishable characters" and let a structural check stand in for it; the card should have required a pixel-level check plus a human look. Plan/card defect, not only a developer one.
8. Fixture DSL is "v1" throughout the kit; the tool is at v2 with an addendum — fine, but the identity block must report supported versions.
