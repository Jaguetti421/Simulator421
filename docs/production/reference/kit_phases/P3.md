## Exit evidence

- Complete Standard100 plus 36 wildlife, 28 recipes, migration and legal finale run through the same host.
- All non-Story production scenes and 100 varied Standard seeds terminate by 36000 ticks without illegal effects, duplication or deadlock.
- Pre-cutoff winner target of at least 95 percent is a reported tuning gate, distinct from correctness; passive coalitions have separate fixtures.
- Supported Custom commands, two commandments, three whispers and scenario provenance are complete.
- Reference-machine performance and urgent response remain valid at full population.

The final P3 integration packet prepares templates/GATE_EVIDENCE.md with exact candidate hashes and actual reports. A failed criterion requires a repair packet followed by affected retests; a repair plan alone cannot pass. Use PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/HUMAN_REQUIRED honestly. Gate evidence is invalidated when later changes affect its behavior; A0 records the targeted revalidation before carrying a gate forward.

## Stop and next step

Agents stop after their assigned packet, even when the next dependency is ready. A0 presents a reviewable phase candidate to Jani and asks explicit permission for the next phase/baseline. Resume of an incomplete authorized packet is allowed; beginning the next packet is a new dispatch. P6 approval never authorizes uploading or publishing the game. Optional PX may remain deferred without changing core scope.
